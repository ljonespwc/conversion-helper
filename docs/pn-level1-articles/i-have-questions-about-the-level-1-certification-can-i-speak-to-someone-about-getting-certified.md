# I have questions about the Level 1 Certification. Can I speak to someone about getting certified?

[Skip to main content](https://precisionnutrition.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406796320276-I-have-questions-about-the-Level-1-Certification-Can-I-speak-to-someone-about-getting-certified#main-content)

Becoming certified as a nutrition coach is a big step and it's natural to have questions before you register for Level 1 Certification.

Our Certification Advisors are here to help during this decision-making process.They are all certified themselves and can respond to any questions or worries you might have, and give you a clear picture of what to expect.

**You can connect with a Certification Advisor in whatever way works best for you:**

- **Live chat** \- Stop by the live chat feature on our [website](https://www.precisionnutrition.com/nutrition-certification-level-1-register-now) to chat with a Certification Advisor right away
- **Email** \- Send your questions to [certificationadvisor@precisionnutrition.com](mailto:certificationadvisor@precisionnutrition.com)
- **Phone call** \- Schedule a call to discuss your questions in detail

Our advisors are experienced coaches who understand the certification process inside and out. They can help you determine if the program is right for your goals, answer questions about pricing and payment options, and guide you through the enrollment process.

**We're happy to hear from you!** Whether you have specific questions about the curriculum, want to understand the time commitment, or just want to learn more about what it's like to be a PN-certified coach, our team is here to support you in making the best decision for your career and goals.
