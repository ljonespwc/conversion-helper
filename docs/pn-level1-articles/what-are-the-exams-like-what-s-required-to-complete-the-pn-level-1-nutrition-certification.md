# What are the exams like / what's required to complete the PN Level 1 Nutrition Certification?

Instead of a single final exam, there are 20 short exams for each chapter of the book—each one a mix of multiple choice and true/false questions.

Each exam has 10 questions, which means there are 200 cumulative questions in the course. A passing grade means answering at least 150 out of the 200 total questions (a cumulative 75%).  While you can't retake any single chapter exam, you'll have the opportunity to retest at the end of the program if you've not achieved a cumulative score of 75% or higher.

The exams are delivered online at your convenience, open book and not timed, so you can take the time you need to get through them.
