# How long does it take to complete the Level 1 Nutrition Certification?

**The PN Level 1 Nutrition Certification is completely self-paced, so you can take as much or as little time as you like to complete it.**

**Recommended pace:** The pace that seems to work best for most of our students is completing **one chapter per week**. This includes:

- Reading the chapter
- Reviewing the online video
- Answering the workbook questions
- Taking the 10-question end-of-chapter exam

**Time commitment:** If you follow this structure, you can expect to spend **3-5 hours per week** on the certification materials.

**Total timeline:** Since there are **20 total chapters**, you'd earn your PN Nutrition Certification in about **20 weeks** (approximately 5 months) following the recommended pace.

**Flexibility is key:** There is no course schedule or deadlines. You can:

- Move faster if you want to finish sooner
- Take more time if your schedule is busy
- Take breaks when needed and pick up where you left off

**Start coaching immediately:** You don't need to wait to get certified to start coaching nutrition. Since you'll be learning and practicing from day one, you can start helping your clients immediately as you progress through the program.

This flexible approach makes the certification accessible whether you're working full-time, have family commitments, or just prefer to learn at your own pace.
