# Do I need to recertify once I've earned my PN Level 1 Nutrition Coach credential?

[Skip to main content](https://precisionnutrition.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/31069666644756-Do-I-need-to-recertify-once-I-ve-earned-my-PN-Level-1-Nutrition-Coach-credential#main-content)

Yes, you do need to recertify your PN Level 1 Nutrition Certification every two years.

**Here's how it works:**

**Recertification timeline:** Your Level 1 Nutrition Coaching Certification is valid for 2 years from your completion date. After that, you'll need to renew to maintain your "PN Certified" status.

**Recertification process:** To renew, you simply need to pass a 50-question multiple choice exam every two years. This exam is **completely free** for all graduates, delivered to your course Today page 90 days to your expiry date. No continuing education credits are required.

**Why recertification matters:**

- Keeps you on the cutting edge of the field with current best practices
- Demonstrates your commitment to continued learning
- Helps you continue delivering the best possible results for your clients
- Maintains your credibility and professional standing

**Timing:** As long as you complete the recertification process before your certification expires, you'll maintain your status as "PN Certified." We'll remind you when it's time to renew.

**The good news:** The recertification exam is straightforward and covers the core principles you learned in the program. Since it's free and you can take it online, it's a simple way to stay current and maintain your professional credentials.

This requirement ensures that all PN Certified coaches stay up-to-date with the latest evidence-based practices and continue representing the high standards that make the PN certification so respected in the industry.
