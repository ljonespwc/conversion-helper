# Are there any prerequisites for Level 1 Certification?

There are no prerequisites required to take the PN Level 1 Nutrition Certification.

**The program is designed for everyone—whether you're an aspiring, new, or seasoned coach.** The PN Nutrition Certification is your springboard to a deeper understanding of nutrition, the authority to coach it, and the ability to turn what you know into lasting results.

#### **Who can benefit:**

**Current health and fitness professionals** \- Many of our students currently work in or operate a health and fitness practice and are simply seeking a proven method to use with clients who want to improve their nutrition habits. This includes:

- Personal trainers and strength coaches
- Group exercise instructors
- Naturopaths, chiropractors, physicians, dentists, nurses
- Other healthcare professionals who want to communicate better with patients

**Career changers and newcomers** \- You don't have to currently be a health or fitness professional to benefit from the PN Nutrition Certification. Many people join the program as their first major step into the industry, often while still in the job they plan to leave for a career in coaching.

**People focused on their own health** \- Many students join to gain a deeper understanding of nutrition science and behavior change for themselves and their families. The knowledge and tools you'll learn can transform your own relationship with food and health, whether or not you plan to coach others.

**What matters most:** The single biggest factor for success isn't how much you know about nutrition, whether you were a good student in school, or how much time you've spent in the health and fitness industry.

**The key to success comes down to one question: Are you passionate about helping people lead healthier lives?**

If you can answer "yes," then this program is for you. We're educators and coaches who are used to working with people who have a wide range of abilities and experiences, and we designed our curriculum to account for this.
