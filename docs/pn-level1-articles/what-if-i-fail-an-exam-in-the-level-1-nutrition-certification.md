# What if I fail an exam in the Level 1 Nutrition Certification?

It may come as a surprise, but the chapter exams for the PN Level 1 Nutrition Certification are completely open-book.

You see, we know from experience that when it comes to expert-level coaching, it's not about memorizing every detail in some textbook. It's about knowing (and teaching students) how to help clients on a daily basis.

Your score for the course is cumulative, so you could do very poorly on a few exams and still pass the course as long as you answer 150 out of 200 questions correctly by the end of the course.

The exams aren't timed, and they are open book (just like when coaching actual clients, you can always look something up in your materials), so we encourage all students to take their time.

Additionally, when you're enrolled, we'll be right there to help with your questions about the material.

You'll also find plenty of recommendations and advice in our PN Coaches Community Facebook group —based on your peers' previous experiences.

While unlikely, if you don't happen to earn 150 out of 200 points in the program, you can write a re-do exam at the end of the program. You'll get 5 chances to earn 75% or greater on this exam.

Rest assured, we have your back—and so do your peers! If you find you need help with the program you'll have access to our private online community of PN's top experts and thousands of like-minded coaches from around the world, all there to help you succeed.
