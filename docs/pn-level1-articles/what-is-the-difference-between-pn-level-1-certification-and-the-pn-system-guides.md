# What is the difference between PN Level 1 Certification and the PN System Guides?

[Skip to main content](https://precisionnutrition.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404375691668-What-is-the-difference-between-PN-Level-1-Certification-and-the-PN-System-Guides#main-content)

The PN System and PN Level 1 Certification serve completely different purposes, even though they're both based on our proven methodology.

#### The PN System

**What it is:** A self-guided nutrition program for individuals who want to improve their own health and eating habits.

**Who it's for:**

- People who want to take control of their own nutrition and health
- Those tired of confusing diets and want a science-based approach
- Individuals looking to make sustainable lifestyle changes for themselves

**What you get:**

- 11 printed guides shipped to your door (Success Guide, Diet Guide, Quick Start Guide, Super Shake recipes, 5-Minute Meals, etc.)
- Self-directed program focused on personal nutrition improvement
- Based on our proven methods tested with over 150,000 clients
- No coaching training or professional credentials

**Goal:** Transform your own relationship with food and health using our proven system. Learn more here: [https://www.precisionnutrition.com/books](https://www.precisionnutrition.com/books)

#### PN Level 1 Nutrition Certification (Professional Training)

**What it is:** A comprehensive professional certification program that trains you to become a qualified nutrition coach.

**Who it's for:**

- Current health and fitness professionals wanting to add nutrition coaching
- People looking to start a career in nutrition coaching
- Anyone who wants to help others improve their health professionally

**What you get:**

- 3 physical textbooks (nearly 700 pages) + digital access
- 20 animated video seminars
- Professional coaching tools and assessments
- PN Pro Calculator access
- Workbook and study guide
- Real-world case studies
- Professional certification and credentials
- Access to coaching business training
- Eligibility for continuing education credits

**Goal:** Become a qualified, confident nutrition coach who can help others achieve lasting health transformations.

#### Key Differences:

- **PN System:** Personal use only, focuses on your own health journey
- **Level 1 Certification:** Professional training to coach and help others
- **PN System:** One-time purchase, self-directed learning
- **Level 1 Certification:** Comprehensive education with professional credentials and ongoing support

**The bottom line:** Choose the PN System if you want to improve your own nutrition. Choose the Level 1 Certification if you want to help others as a nutrition coach.
