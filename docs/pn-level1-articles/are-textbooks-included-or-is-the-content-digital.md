# Are textbooks included or is the content digital?

The PN Level 1 Nutrition Certification includes both physical textbooks AND digital content—giving you the best of both worlds.

#### Physical materials shipped to you:

- **3 Unit textbooks beautifully packaged in a box set** \- Nearly 700 pages of the latest research in nutrition science and behavior-change psychology. Easy to carry from your house, to the car, to the gym, so you can study anywhere!
- **A Workbook and Study Guide** \- With exercises and thought experiments to help prepare you for the end-of-chapter exams

#### Digital content accessed online:

- **Digital textbook reader** \- Accessed through your online PN Level 1 dashboard for convenience and portability
- **20 animated instructional video seminars** \- To help you better understand and reinforce each chapter
- **Chapter exams** \- 10-question exams for each of the 20 chapters to test your knowledge and ensure you're mastering the material
- **Real-world case studies** \- Drawn from our work with over 150,000 clients to help you practice and retain concepts
- **Over 40 premium coaching tools** \- Assessment forms and questionnaires to help you learn more about your clients
- **PN Pro Calculator access** \- Built specifically for L1 students and grads with advanced customization options
- **Active learning tools and strategies** \- To help you use what you're learning in the real world right away

#### Why both formats work great:

- **Physical books** are perfect for deep study, note-taking, and reference without screen fatigue
- **Digital access** gives you the flexibility to study anywhere on your phone, tablet, or computer
- **Video content** helps reinforce complex concepts with visual learning

This hybrid approach ensures you can learn in whatever way works best for you, whether you prefer the tactile experience of physical books or the convenience of digital access.
