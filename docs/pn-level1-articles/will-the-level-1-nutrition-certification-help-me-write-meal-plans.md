# Will the Level 1 Nutrition Certification help me write meal plans?

**The Level 1 Certification will teach you something even better than traditional meal plans—flexible, sustainable nutrition frameworks that work in real life.**

**Here's the thing about meal plans:** What we've learned from coaching over 150,000 clients is that for almost all people, rigid meal plans don't actually work. They're too prescriptive—eat _this exact thing_, in _this exact amount_, at _this exact time_. When life happens (and it always does), these rigid plans fall apart.

Plus, being prescriptive with nutrition falls outside the scope of practice for nutrition coaches. "Prescribing" is limited to registered dietitians and medical professionals.

**Instead, you'll learn the PN approach** that gives people the tools and education to build their own healthy meals consistently using sound dietary principles.

**What you'll learn to create:**

**PN Plate Templates** \- Visual guides that show clients how to build balanced meals using three different approaches:

- Balanced plate (moderate everything)
- Low-carb, high-fat plate
- Low-fat, high-carb plate

**Hand Portion System** \- A simple way for clients to portion meals using their own hands as measuring tools, making it super practical and personalized to their body size

**Pro Calculator** \- Simply enter your client's details, goals, and eating preferences and get a beautiful, personalized eating guide within minutes (this tool comes with your certification!)

**Why this approach gets better results:**

- Clients can eat foods they actually enjoy and that fit their lifestyle
- No measuring cups, food scales, or rigid schedules required
- Works whether they're eating at home, restaurants, or on the go
- Teaches sustainable skills rather than temporary restrictions
- Empowers clients to make better food choices on their own
- Flexible enough to work with any dietary preference

The program focuses on nutrition education and behavior change coaching skills, giving you the tools and systems to coach clients through the process of eating better. You'll get clear guidelines to ensure you stay in scope while seeing better results with your clients.
