# If I finish my Level 1 Certification sooner, do I still owe all 12 monthly payments?

[Skip to main content](https://precisionnutrition.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360062098332-If-I-finish-my-Level-1-Certification-sooner-do-I-still-owe-all-12-monthly-payments#main-content)

Your payment plan and study approach are completely separate. While the program is self-paced (meaning you can take more or less time if you need to), your payment plan is a total of 12 monthly payments. That means that no matter how fast you complete the certification, you are still responsible for making a total of 12 monthly payments.

Since it is a payment plan rather than a subscription, you will continue to have access to your course once it is paid off without having to make any further payments. So you will always have the course material to refer back to.
