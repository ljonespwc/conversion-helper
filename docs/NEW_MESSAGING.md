# EasyAsk — New Messaging

Approved positioning statements and messaging for use across the app.

---

## App Header Subtitle
**"Your content. Your rules. Zero hallucinations."**

## Landing Page Hero
**"Nobody reads your sales page. Now they don't have to."**

## Feature: Page-Scoped Knowledge
**"Right Context, Every Page"**
- "Scrape pages, upload docs, and assign what the AI knows — per page or across your whole site. Broad or focused, you decide."
- "Give it your entire knowledge base, or just one sales deck. The AI answers from exactly what you allow — nothing more, nothing less."

## Feature: Cross-Product Intelligence
**"One Page. The Whole Story."**
- "Visitors ask about products that aren't on the page they're looking at. That's not a problem — it's purchase intent. EasyAsk answers from everything you give it: your full suite, your pricing, how products complement each other. The page is the starting point. The conversation goes wherever the buyer wants."

## Feature: Real-Time Objection Handling
**"One Unanswered Question Kills the Deal"**
- "Your visitor is on your pricing page right now, ready to buy — but they have one question. If they have to search for it, email you, or 'come back later,' they won't. Answer it now."

## Feature: Consultative Selling
**"AI That Sells the Way Your Best Rep Does"**
- "Tag any page as a sales page, and EasyAsk shifts from support mode to consultative selling. It picks up on buying cues — questions about pricing, comparisons, implementation — and responds with answers that address objections while asking questions that qualify intent. It doesn't push. It guides. The same way a great salesperson would: answer, then ask, then advance."

## Feature: Smart Escalation
**"It Knows What It Doesn't Know"**
- "Most chatbots guess when they're stumped. EasyAsk doesn't. When the AI can't answer from your approved content, it tells the visitor honestly — then captures their email and question before they leave. Your team gets a warm lead with the exact question that needs answering. No hallucination. No lost prospect."

## Feature: Comprehension Tools
**"They Don't Just Read It. They Get It."**
- "Built-in tools let visitors translate, simplify, summarize, or define terms — right inside the chat. Complex product? Technical specs? Long-form content? Your visitors can break it down on their own terms, in their own language. No more bouncing because the page was too dense to digest."

## Landing Page Audit (current page at src/app/page.tsx)

### What's strong — keep or evolve
- **"Your website is a monologue. Visitors want a conversation."** — Best line on the page. Sharp, memorable.
- **"Every silent bounce is a conversation that never happened."** — Great emotional hook.
- **The manifesto section** ("The end of scrolling") is genuinely good. The "end of scroll-first websites" vision feels authentic and founder-voiced. Worth keeping in some form.
- **Use cases section** is concrete and specific ("4 pages to explain, 2 minutes to decide"). Effective.
- **Page structure is solid**: problem → solution → differentiators → use cases → how it works → CTA. Good bones.

### What's weak — replace with new messaging
- **Hero is generic.** "Give your site the power of conversation" could be any chatbot. Doesn't say what's different about EasyAsk. Replace with approved hero: "Nobody reads your sales page. Now they don't have to."
- **"What makes EasyAsk deliciously different"** — "deliciously" feels forced. The differentiators themselves are solid but the heading undermines them.
- **"If this is you, we should talk"** — vague. Doesn't earn the click.
- **"Built for sales, not support"** (final CTA) — contradicts the product, which does both. New messaging handles this better (sell on sales pages, support on support pages).
- **Stats section is odd** — "0 visibility into visitor intent" reads more like a problem statement than a stat. "24/7" and "60%" are fine but generic.
- **No mention of comprehension tools** (Translate, Simplify, etc.) — a real differentiator completely absent from the page.
- **No mention of consultative selling** — the biggest differentiator vs. other chat widgets isn't surfaced at all.
