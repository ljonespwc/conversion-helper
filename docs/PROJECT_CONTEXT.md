# EasyAsk Project Context

## Overview

EasyAsk is a text-based AI chat widget that helps website visitors find answers through natural conversation. The widget appears as a small button on the page and opens a modal chat interface.

Answers are grounded in the customer's own content:

- Scraped website pages
- Uploaded files such as sales decks, case studies, support docs, and technical specs
- Gemini File Search for retrieval

## Core Value Proposition

"Turn browsing into buying. Answer every question, close more sales, with AI that knows your product."

EasyAsk replaces static content with intelligent conversation. It meets prospects where they are in the buying journey, answers objections in real time, and captures intent before they leave.

## Primary Features And Benefits

### Conversational Chat Interface

Visitors type questions and get immediate contextual answers. This is lower friction than forms, search, or reading through long pages.

### Page-Specific Intelligence

Different pages can have different AI context. A pricing page can answer pricing questions, a features page can explain value, and a support page can focus on help.

### Customer Content, AI-Powered

EasyAsk answers from the customer's own information rather than generic model knowledge. Customers can scrape pages or upload files.

### Built To Close Sales

The assistant is designed for conversion:

- Answers product questions in real time
- Handles objections before they become blockers
- Captures purchase intent while visitors are engaged
- Tracks satisfaction and buyer intent signals

### Lead Capture And Escalation

When the assistant cannot fully answer, it can collect an email and question so a human can follow up.

### Conversation Analytics

Admins can see what prospects asked, how satisfied they were, where content gaps exist, and which visitors may be ready to buy.

### Simple Content Management

Admins can scrape pages, upload files, assign content to pages, and manage widget pages without code.

## Use Case Positioning

### B2B SaaS And Complex Products

Complex products can take many pages to explain. Prospects often have only a few minutes to understand enough to keep going.

EasyAsk helps by making dense information conversational, delivering page-relevant answers, and surfacing qualified lead signals.

### High-Consideration Purchases

Prospects compare options and small unanswered questions can become deal-breakers.

EasyAsk answers objections in real time, keeps buyers engaged, and helps identify visitors who are close to taking action.

### Support And FAQ Replacement

FAQ pages are hard to navigate and often go unread.

EasyAsk turns support content into a conversational assistant, reduces repeated tickets, and shows where documentation is missing or unclear.

## Key Differentiators

1. Instant conversational answers
2. Page-specific filtering and context
3. Conversion-oriented conversation flow
4. Smart escalation when the AI cannot answer
5. Grounded answers from customer content, not hallucinated generic knowledge

## Founder Value Drivers

1. People do not read, especially long sales pages on mobile.
2. Real-time objection handling preserves buying momentum.
3. Email escalation captures leads before they bounce.
4. Chat is lower friction than forms, search, or static FAQs.
5. Answers should come only from owned content.
6. Conversation analytics reveal what prospects actually care about.
7. Client care teams should focus on high-value human work.
