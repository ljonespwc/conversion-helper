# Guide to Fixing Mediocre Copy

## The Power of Good Messaging

**The Problem**: Mediocre copy is a missed opportunity. Poor messaging obscures your value, no matter how good your idea is.

**The Solution**: Good messaging is an amplifier that takes something good and makes it great.

---

## Two Types of Copy Problems

### 1. Writing Execution Issues

**Add rhythm by varying sentence structure**
- Mix short and long sentences for dynamic feel
- Read through and mark sentences to make longer or shorter
- Rhythm gives writing good pace ("long but felt fast")

**Trim excess fat**
- Say it in fewer words
- Remove boring content
- Cut unnecessary backstory
- Start right before the action

**Strengthen your claims**
- Add data points and surprising statistics
- Include numbers to support your point
- Let readers reach conclusions themselves

**Improve word choice**
- Swap weak nouns and verbs for stronger ones
- Eliminate adverbs
- Remove unnecessary descriptors

**Write like a human**
- Avoid corporate speak
- Sound natural, not like a business manual
- Avoid double negatives (they're confusing)

### 2. Underlying Idea Issues

**Clarify your core message**
- Know what you want to say first
- Writing is just the vessel for your idea
- No amount of good writing fixes a missing idea

**Complete your thought**
- Finish the "knock-knock joke"
- Don't leave ideas incomplete
- Provide the payoff

**Create narrative arc**
- Even tweets need beginning, middle, end
- Ensure standalone completeness
- Build tension and resolution

**Plant positive ideas**
- Avoid "I am not a crook" syndrome
- Focus on what you DO want people to think
- Don't inception negative concepts

**Show your work**
- State assumptions and rationale explicitly
- Share thought process behind recommendations
- Enable productive feedback conversations

**Add unique value**
- Teach something people don't know (Scarce + Relevant = Valuable)
- Share what's surprising and true
- Make additive contributions, not summaries

**Take a clear stance**
- Have a spiky point of view rooted in evidence
- Make it something others can disagree with
- Avoid wishy-washy positions

**Include clear call-to-action**
- Tell readers exactly what to do
- Give reasons for an enthusiastic "yes"
- Remember: messaging drives customer action

## Email Quality Checklist

Before sending, verify your email:

**Persona Alignment**
- [ ] Matches the target persona's needs
- [ ] Addresses persona pain points sufficiently

**Engagement Elements**
- [ ] Has compelling hook to draw readers in
- [ ] Tells readers specific benefits they'll get
- [ ] Gets readers excited about becoming a nutrition coach

**Value & Trust**
- [ ] Helps the reader in a meaningful way
- [ ] Instills more trust in Precision Nutrition
- [ ] Matches modern reading habits of target persona

**Message Quality**
- [ ] Addresses writing execution and idea issues above
- [ ] Has clear call-to-action
- [ ] Provides unique, valuable content
